**Title**
Floorwalker: spend controls between AI agents and PayPal (PayPal AI Hackathon)

**Description**
Every refund checked. Fewer taps every week.

Floorwalker sits between AI agents and a business's PayPal account: every refund or payout is checked against PayPal's records and your rules, and it learns from your decisions when it no longer needs to ask you.

This video replays one recorded live run against the PayPal sandbox: real refunds and payouts through the PayPal Agent Toolkit and the PayPal REST APIs, a push notification and one-tap approval on a phone, a prompt injection hidden in an email, a payment-redirection scam, a hijacked agent that gets frozen, an outside agent calling Floorwalker's API with its own key and limits, rules tested on past activity before going live, and a tamper-evident audit log.
