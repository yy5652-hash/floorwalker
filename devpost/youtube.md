**Title**
Allowance: spend controls between AI agents and PayPal (PayPal AI Hackathon)

**Description**
Give your AI agent an allowance, not your wallet.

Allowance sits between an AI support agent and a business's PayPal account. Every refund or payout the agent tries is checked against PayPal's own records and the owner's rules, then reviewed by AI: it goes through, waits for one-tap approval, or is blocked before it reaches PayPal.

This video replays one recorded live run against the PayPal sandbox: real refunds and payouts through the PayPal Agent Toolkit and the PayPal REST APIs, a prompt injection hidden in an email, a payment-redirection scam, and a hijacked agent.

Built with the PayPal Agent Toolkit, PayPal Orders, Payments and Payouts APIs (sandbox), Gemini, AG Grid, and MCP.
